The allure of turning everyday spending into travel rewards remains strong for US travelers. The Chase Sapphire Preferred Card, a long-standing favorite with a $95 annual fee, faces the question of its continued value in 2025. Its reputation for flexible Ultimate Rewards points, travel protections, and strong transfer partners is well-established, earning it accolades like a 5.0 rating from Forbes Advisor and NerdWallet's Best All-Purpose Travel Rewards Credit Card for 2025. The card occupies a "sweet spot," offering premium benefits without the hefty fees of cards like the Chase Sapphire Reserve ($550 annual fee).

A compelling factor in 2025 is the welcome bonus: 100,000 bonus points after spending $5,000 in the first three months. This offer, potentially expiring May 15, 2025, is described as the best in years and is worth $1,250 via Chase Travel or more if transferred. This significantly boosts its first-year appeal.

## <a id="earning-power"></a>2. Earning Power: Racking Up Ultimate Rewards with the Sapphire Preferred
The Chase Sapphire Preferred offers several ways to earn Ultimate Rewards points. Cardholders get 5x total points on travel purchased through Chase Travel (excluding hotel purchases qualifying for the $50 hotel credit). Dining, including takeout and eligible delivery services, earns 3x points. Select streaming services and online grocery purchases (excluding Target, Walmart, and wholesale clubs) also earn 3x points.

For travel booked outside the Chase portal, the card offers 2x points on all other travel purchases, covering a broad range of expenses like direct airline/hotel bookings, rideshares, and public transit. All other purchases earn 1x point per dollar.

A 10% Anniversary Points Bonus is awarded annually, equaling 10% of total purchases from the previous year. For $25,000 spent, this means 2,500 bonus points, valued at $31.25 via Chase Travel or potentially $52 with partner transfers.

Partnerships in 2025 include:
-   Lyft: 5x total points on Lyft rides (April 1, 2025 - September 30, 2027).
-   Peloton: 5x total points on eligible Peloton equipment/accessory purchases over $150 (max 25,000 bonus points, through December 31, 2027).

The earning structure encourages using the Chase portal but still rewards direct bookings, appealing to a wider range of travelers.

Transferring points 1:1 to airline and hotel partners often yields greater value.
-   **Airline Partners for US travelers**: United MileagePlus, Southwest Airlines Rapid Rewards, JetBlue TrueBlue, British Airways Executive Club (for Oneworld/American Airlines), Air Canada Aeroplan, Flying Blue AIR FRANCE KLM, Emirates Skywards, Singapore Airlines KrisFlyer, Virgin Atlantic Flying Club, Iberia Plus, Aer Lingus AerClub.
-   **Hotel Partners**: World of Hyatt, Marriott Bonvoy, IHG Rewards Club. World of Hyatt is often cited for exceptional value. Experts value transferred points higher, around 1.6 to 2.05 cents each.

## <a id="who-shouldnt-get"></a>6. Who shouldn't get this card?
Not ideal for those who won't use the $50 hotel credit, prefer simple cash back, have very low annual spend, are subject to Chase's 5/24 rule, ineligible for the bonus (received one in the past 48 months), or need premium perks like lounge access (found on Sapphire Reserve).

## <a id="competition"></a>7. How the Sapphire Preferred Stacks Up: A Look at the Competition

### Chase Sapphire Preferred vs. Chase Sapphire Reserve
The Preferred ($95 fee) offers a $50 hotel credit (via Chase Travel) and 1.25 cpp in the portal. The Reserve ($550 fee) has a $300 general travel credit, 1.5 cpp in the portal, lounge access, and a Global Entry/TSA PreCheck credit. The Reserve's effective fee is $250 vs. Preferred's $45. Many find the Preferred a better value unless Reserve's premium perks are fully utilized.

### Head-to-Head with Key Competitors (Sub-$100 Annual Fee Category):
-   **Capital One Venture Rewards Credit Card** ($95 fee): Offers 2x miles on everything and a Global Entry/TSA PreCheck credit. Sapphire Preferred has better bonus categories, a 1.25 cpp portal value, the $50 hotel credit, stronger US transfer partners (Hyatt, United), and primary auto CDW (Venture's is secondary).

## <a id="application-tips"></a>8. Navigating the Application: What to Know Before You Apply
Chase's 5/24 rule is key: likely denial if you've opened 5+ credit cards (any bank) in 24 months.

Sapphire welcome bonus eligibility: Not for current Sapphire cardholders or those who received a Sapphire bonus in the last 48 months.

A "Good" to "Excellent" credit score (often 670+, more commonly 700s+) is recommended.

## <a id="verdict"></a>9. The 2025 Verdict: Is the Chase Sapphire Preferred Still a Must-Have for US Travelers?
The Chase Sapphire Preferred largely justifies its $95 fee in 2025.

### Strengths in the 2025 Landscape:
The 100,000-point welcome bonus is exceptional. Strong earning rates (5x Chase Travel, 3x dining/online grocery/streaming, 2x other travel). Valuable redemptions (1.25 cpp via Chase Travel, 1:1 transfers to partners like Hyatt, United, Southwest). The $50 annual hotel credit effectively makes the fee $45. Comprehensive travel insurance (primary auto CDW). Valuable DoorDash benefits and 5x on Lyft. No foreign transaction fees.

### Weaknesses or Considerations:
The $95 annual fee is a factor. Top 5x travel earn requires using the Chase Travel portal. Lacks airport lounge access. Pay Yourself Back is currently limited to charities for CSP. Strict application rules (5/24, 48-month bonus).

### Final Expert Recommendation:
**YES, the Chase Sapphire Preferred is still worth its $95 annual fee in 2025 for many US travelers.** It's highly recommended for:
-   New applicants who can meet the 100,000-point bonus spend.
-   Those who travel and can use the $50 hotel credit.
-   Travelers maximizing rewards via partner transfers (especially Hyatt, United, Southwest).
-   Those valuing comprehensive travel insurance (primary auto CDW).
-   Regular DoorDash users.
-   People seeking a strong "starter" premium travel card.

**NO, it might not be the best fit for:**
-   Infrequent travelers unlikely to use travel benefits.
-   Those wanting simple cash back.
-   Very low annual spenders.
-   Travelers prioritizing lounge access (consider Sapphire Reserve).
-   Applicants ineligible due to Chase rules.

The Sapphire Preferred's strength lies in its overall package of rewards, credits, and robust insurance at an effective $45 fee (after hotel credit), making it a versatile and compelling choice. Recent benefit enhancements have kept it competitive.
