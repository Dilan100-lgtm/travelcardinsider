## <a id="introduction-bevy"></a>I. The $250 Question: An Introduction

The Bevy holds a peculiar spot in the extensive Marriott co-branded card lineup. It was seemingly created to fill a gap for the regular Marriott guest who wants more than the basics but isn't ready for the top-tier **Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant®** card. The most significant point of contention is its break from a long-standing industry norm: the absence of an *automatic* annual Free Night Award, a standard benefit for hotel cards at this price point. Instead, the Bevy makes you earn it.

## <a id="deconstructing-value-bevy"></a>II. Deconstructing the Bevy's Value
To determine if the card is a smart financial move, we need to treat it like an investment.

### The Welcome Offer: A Powerful, but Temporary, Boost
The card’s most attractive feature is its welcome offer. New cardholders can earn a significant number of Marriott Bonvoy points, which can easily cover the $250 annual fee for the first year. However, this is a one-time benefit. The long-term decision to keep the card must be justified by its ongoing perks. For the current offer, see the [official American Express website](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/card/marriott-bonvoy-bevy/).

### The Earning Engine: A Tale of Two Multipliers
The Bevy’s rewards structure is designed to accumulate points through both travel and everyday spending.

**Table 1: Earning Points with the Bonvoy Bevy**

| Spending Category | Points per $1 | Notes |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| Hotels in Marriott Bonvoy program | **6X** |  |
| Restaurants (Worldwide) & U.S. Supermarkets | **4X** | Rate drops to 2X after $15,000 in combined purchases per year. |
| All Other Eligible Purchases | **2X** |  |

Using a conservative valuation of 0.8 cents per point, these rates translate to a return of **4.8%** at Marriott properties, **3.2%** on dining/groceries (within the cap), and a modest **1.6%** on all other purchases. The 3.2% return is respectable but is outmatched by many flexible rewards cards.

## <a id="perks-package-bevy"></a>III. The Perks Package: Status vs. Hurdles

### Automatic Gold Elite Status: Still Golden in 2025?
Cardholders are granted complimentary [Marriott Bonvoy Gold Elite status](https://www.marriott.com/loyalty/member-benefits/gold.mi). Key benefits include a 25% points bonus on stays and a 2 p.m. late checkout. While nice, the truly transformative perks like free breakfast and guaranteed lounge access are reserved for Platinum Elite and above.

### The Free Night Award: Marriott's $15,000 Gamble
The card's most debated feature is its Free Night Award (FNA). You can earn one FNA, redeemable for a stay worth up to 50,000 points, but **only after spending $15,000** on the card in a calendar year.

This creates a high opportunity cost. That same $15,000 on a no-annual-fee 2% cash-back card would yield a guaranteed $300. This means the "free" night's effective cost is the $250 fee plus the ~$300 in rewards you miss out on by not using a better card.

## <a id="competitor-comparison-bevy"></a>IV. A Crowded Field: Bevy vs. Rivals
The Bevy's questionable value is clearest when compared to its direct competitors.

**Table 2: Bonvoy Bevy vs. Key Competitors**

| Card | Annual Fee | Key Annual Perk |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Marriott Bonvoy Bevy™ ($250)** | **$250** | **Earn** a 50k Free Night Award after **$15,000 spend**. |
| [**Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant™**](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/card/marriott-bonvoy-brilliant/) | **$650** | **Automatic** 85k Free Night Award + Platinum Status + $300 Dining Credit. |
| [**Marriott Bonvoy Boundless®**](https://creditcards.chase.com/marriott/cardmember/boundless) | **$95** | **Automatic** 35k Free Night Award. |

The **Boundless** card offers a guaranteed annual free night that easily exceeds its modest $95 fee, making it a value champion. The **Brilliant** card, while expensive, provides clear, high-value perks that can justify its cost for frequent travelers. The Bevy is stuck in the middle.

## <a id="final-verdict-bevy"></a>VI. Final Verdict & Recommendations

### <a id="ideal-cardholder-bevy"></a>A. The Ideal Bevy Cardholder Profile:
- Is a committed Marriott loyalist with many separate, short, paid hotel stays each year.
- Can easily and naturally spend $15,000 on dining and at U.S. supermarkets annually.
- Values Gold Elite status but is unwilling to pay the high fee for the Brilliant card.
- Is not focused on maximizing every single dollar of spending.

### <a id="recommendations-bevy"></a>B. Actionable Recommendations for Everyone Else:
- **For the Occasional Marriott Traveler:** The [Marriott Bonvoy Boundless® Card](https://creditcards.chase.com/marriott/cardmember/boundless) is the better choice.
- **For the Serious Marriott Loyalist:** The [Marriott Bonvoy Brilliant® Card](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/card/marriott-bonvoy-brilliant/) is the clear winner.
- **For the Flexible Traveler:** A general travel rewards card like the [Chase Sapphire Preferred® Card](https://creditcards.chase.com/rewards-credit-cards/sapphire/preferred) offers greater overall value.

### <a id="eeta-bevy"></a>C. Editor's Essential Takeaways (EETA)
For the vast majority of U.S. travelers, the Marriott Bonvoy Bevy™ American Express® Card is **not worth its $250 annual fee in 2025**. The card is stuck in a strategic middle ground, outdone on value by its less expensive counterpart and outclassed on perks by its premium sibling. While its welcome bonus is tempting for a one-year trial, its long-term place in a savvy traveler's wallet is very difficult to justify.
