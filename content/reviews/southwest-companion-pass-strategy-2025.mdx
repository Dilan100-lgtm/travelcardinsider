## <a id="what-is-it"></a>I. What is the Southwest Companion Pass?
Southwest Airlines lays out two official ways for Rapid Rewards® members to earn the Companion Pass in a single calendar year:

- Fly 100 qualifying one-way flights.
- Earn 135,000 Companion Pass qualifying points.

Let's be realistic. Flying 100 times in a year is a feat reserved for the most elite road warriors. That’s why the most strategic and accessible path for nearly everyone is earning those qualifying points through the smart use of credit cards.

The real magic is in the pass's validity period. Once you earn it, the Companion Pass is valid for the rest of the calendar year you qualify in, **plus the entire following calendar year**. Earn the pass in February 2025, and you’ll be flying BOGO until December 31, 2026.

Qualifying points include points from Southwest flights, partner activity, and most importantly, all points earned from Southwest Rapid Rewards® Credit Cards, including welcome bonuses. Points that do not count are those transferred from partners like Chase Ultimate Rewards, purchased points, or points gifted from another member. You can read the full terms on the [official Companion Pass page](https://www.southwest.com/rapid-rewards/tiers/companion-pass/).

## <a id="secret-formula"></a>II. The Secret Formula: Earning with Two Cards
The most efficient way to rack up 135,000 points is by earning the welcome bonuses from two Southwest co-branded credit cards in the same calendar year. This isn't just a suggestion; it’s a strategy dictated by the rules from the card issuer, Chase Bank.

Here's what you need to know:
- You can only hold **one personal** Southwest credit card at a time.
- You are not eligible for a new personal card bonus if you’ve received one within the last 24 months.

These two rules mean you can’t simply open two personal cards. **The proven strategy is to open one personal Southwest card and one business Southwest card.** The combined welcome bonuses will get you the vast majority of the way there.

Plus, as a cardholder, you get an automatic **10,000-point boost** toward Companion Pass qualification each year, effectively reducing your target to 125,000 points.

## <a id="chase-524-rule"></a>IV. The Biggest Hurdle: Navigating the Chase 5/24 Rule
Before you apply, you must know your status with the Chase 5/24 rule. This is an unwritten policy where Chase will likely deny you for most of their cards if you have opened **five or more new personal credit card accounts in the last 24 months.**

To check your 5/24 status:
1. Get your free credit reports from [AnnualCreditReport.com](https://www.annualcreditreport.com).
2. Count every personal credit card account opened in the past 24 months.
3. If your count is four or less, you are under 5/24 and ready to apply.

## <a id="timing-is-everything"></a>V. Timing is Everything: Your 2025-2026 Game Plan
This is the most critical part of the strategy. **All 135,000 points must be earned in the same calendar year.**

1.  **Apply (October – November 2024):** Apply for your chosen personal and business cards.
2.  **Careful Spending (November – December 2024):** Work towards the minimum spending requirements, but **DO NOT** meet the full threshold before your December 2024 statement closes.
3.  **The Final Push (On or after January 1, 2025):** Make the final purchases to meet the minimum spend on both cards.
4.  **Points Post (Late January – February 2025):** After your January 2025 statement closes, the welcome bonuses will transfer to your account.
5.  **Companion Pass Earned!** Your pass will be valid from that day through **December 31, 2026.**

## <a id="pro-tips"></a>VII. A Pro's Guide to Using the Pass
Congratulations! Using the pass is simple, but a few pro tips will make it even smoother.

- **Designating Your Companion:** Call Southwest at 1-800-435-9792 to add your first companion to avoid a known online glitch.
- **Booking a Flight:** Book your own flight first, then go to "My Trips" and use the "Add Companion" link. You'll only pay taxes and fees (from $5.60 each way).
- **Re-Pricing Flights:** If a flight price drops, you can get the difference back. You must first cancel your companion's ticket, re-price yours, and then re-add the companion.
- **Changing Companions:** You can change your designated companion up to three times per calendar year.
