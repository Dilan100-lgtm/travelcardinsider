## <a id="june-2025-expiring-bonuses"></a>II. Spotlight on June 2025’s Hottest Expiring Travel Bonuses

For US-based travelers, especially business owners who can leverage their expenses, two offers stand out from the pack. They are both exceptional, and both are disappearing this month.

### <a id="venture-x-business-offer"></a>1. The High-Spend Standout: Capital One Venture X Business

For businesses with significant cash flow, the Capital One Venture X Business card is currently offering its best-ever welcome bonus. This is a powerhouse offer that demands your attention.

* **The Offer:** Earn a massive bonus of up to 350,000 miles. You’ll get 150,000 miles after spending $30,000 in the first 3 months and another 200,000 miles if you spend a total of $200,000 in the first 6 months.
* **Expiration Date:** This strong opportunity ends on **June 9, 2025**.
* **Annual Fee:** $395
* **Unpacking the Value:** At a bare minimum, 350,000 miles are worth $3,500 for travel through the Capital One portal. However, the real magic happens when you transfer those miles to Capital One's airline and hotel partners. By transferring strategically, the value could be worth **$6,475 or more**, based on typical 1.85¢ partner redemptions.
* **Key Perks for US Travelers:**
    * $300 Annual Travel Credit for bookings made through Capital One Travel.
    * 10,000 Anniversary Miles (worth at least $100) every year.
    * Complimentary access to Capital One Lounges and a Priority Pass Select membership.
    * Global Entry or TSA PreCheck Credit (up to $100).
    * No Foreign Transaction Fees.
* **Who is this card for?** This is the perfect card for established businesses with significant planned expenditures that can comfortably meet the minimum spend. It’s a pay-in-full card, so it’s essential to pay your balance every month.
* **More Info/Apply:** [Capital One Venture X Business Official Page](https://www.capitalone.com/small-business/credit-cards/venture-x-business/)
* **Rates & Fees:** See official Capital One site for terms.

### <a id="amex-business-gold-offer"></a>2. The Flexible Powerhouse: American Express Business Gold Card

American Express is a giant in the rewards world, and this targeted offer for the Business Gold card is a prime example of why. It combines a hefty points bonus with a direct cash saving on flights.

* **The Offer:** Earn 100,000 Membership Rewards® points after spending $15,000 on eligible purchases in the first three months. This specific offer also includes a **$500 statement credit** after you spend $2,500 on flights booked directly with airlines or through Amex Travel in the first three months.
* **Expiration Date:** This special offer is set to end on **June 30, 2025**.
* **Annual Fee:** $375
* **Unpacking the Value:** The 100,000 Membership Rewards points are incredibly versatile. When transferred to airline partners, they can be worth around $2,000. Add the $500 flight credit, and you're looking at a total value of **$2,500 to $2,700** from the welcome offer alone.
* **Key Perks for US Travelers:**
    * **4X Points on Your Top 2 Categories** where you spend the most each month (on up to $150,000 in combined purchases per year).

    * Walmart+ Membership Credit.
    * No Foreign Transaction Fees.
* **Who is this card for?** This is a fantastic choice for U.S. businesses with diverse spending that falls into the bonus categories, like marketing agencies, consulting firms, or e-commerce shops.
* **More Info/Apply:** [American Express Business Gold Official Page](https://www.americanexpress.com/us/credit-cards/business/business-gold-card/)

## <a id="advice-before-applying"></a>VI. Before You Click "Apply": A Word of Advice

These offers are incredibly tempting, but it's vital to be strategic. Here are a few things to consider:

* **Spend Responsibly:** Never spend more than you normally would just to hit a bonus.
* **Check Your Credit:** These are premium cards, and you’ll generally need a good to excellent credit score (typically 670+, but often higher).
* **Know the Rules:** American Express has a "once in a lifetime" rule. Capital One can be sensitive to recent credit applications.
* **Plan for the Future:** Make sure the card's ongoing benefits will justify the annual fee for you in year two and beyond.
* **Avoid Interest:** These are rewards cards, not tools for carrying debt. High-interest rates will quickly erase the value of your points.
